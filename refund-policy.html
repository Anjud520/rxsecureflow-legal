Refund Policy

Effective date: September 19, 2026 · Last updated: September 19, 2026

At RxSecureFlow, we want you to be satisfied with our services. This Refund Policy outlines the conditions under which refunds may be issued for payments made through the Service.

1. Subscription Refunds

1.1 Free Plan

The free plan does not involve any payment and therefore is not eligible for refunds.

1.2 Paid Subscriptions

Timeframe Refund Eligibility
Within 7 days of purchase Full refund if the subscription has not been used to send prescriptions or conduct consultations
Within 7 days of purchase Partial refund (minus usage fees) if subscription features were used
After 7 days No refund for the current billing period
Auto-renewal charges Refund available within 48 hours of auto-renewal if cancellation request is submitted

1.3 How to Cancel

  • You can cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings
  • Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period
  • You will continue to have access to paid features until the end of the current period

2. Consultation Fee Refunds

  • Before consultation begins: Full refund if the doctor does not accept or respond within 15 minutes
  • During consultation: No refund for time already consumed; you may end the session at any time
  • Technical failure: If the consultation is interrupted due to a platform error, a pro-rata refund for unused time will be issued
  • Doctor no-show: Full refund if the doctor fails to join the scheduled consultation within 10 minutes

3. Prescription Charges

  • Prescription charges are set by the doctor and are non-refundable once the prescription is unlocked and delivered
  • If a prescription is not delivered due to a platform error, a full refund will be issued
  • If the payment was processed but the prescription was not unlocked, please contact us within 24 hours for resolution

4. Refund Process

  1. Submit a refund request via email to billing@rxsecureflow.com or through the Contact page
  2. Include your account email, transaction ID, and reason for the refund request
  3. We will review your request within 3-5 business days
  4. If approved, the refund will be processed to the original payment method within 7-10 business days

5. Non-Refundable Items

  • Consultation time that has already been consumed
  • Prescriptions that have been successfully delivered and unlocked
  • Subscription periods that have been fully utilized
  • Transaction fees charged by payment gateways (non-recoverable from our end)

6. Disputes

If you disagree with a refund decision, you may escalate the matter by emailing escalations@rxsecureflow.com. We will respond within 10 business days.

7. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to update this Refund Policy at any time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

8. Contact

For refund-related questions, please contact:

  • Email: billing@rxsecureflow.com
  • Website: Contact Page
