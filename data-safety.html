Data Safety & Deletion

Effective date: September 19, 2026 · Last updated: September 19, 2026

This page describes how RxSecureFlow collects, shares, and protects your data, as well as how you can request deletion of your data. This information is provided in compliance with Google Play Store Data Safety requirements.

Data We Collect

Data Type Collected Shared Purpose
Name, email, phone Yes No Account creation and authentication
Health information Yes No Prescriptions and consultations
Financial data (UPI, bank details) Yes Payment processors only Payment processing
Prescriptions and medical records Yes No Medical record management
Consultation chat messages Yes No Doctor-patient communication
Device information Yes No App performance and security
Usage data / analytics Yes No Service improvement

How We Protect Your Data

  • Encryption: All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256)
  • Access control: Role-based access ensures only authorized personnel can access sensitive data
  • Secure payments: Payment information is processed by PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways (Razorpay, Cashfree). We never store full card or bank details
  • Prescription security: Prescriptions are locked until payment is verified, preventing unauthorized access
  • Regular audits: We conduct periodic security assessments and vulnerability scans

Data Sharing

We share data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • With your explicit consent: When you choose to send a prescription via WhatsApp or email
  • Payment processors: Transaction data is shared with Razorpay/Cashfree solely to process payments
  • Legal obligation: When required by law, regulation, or valid legal process

We never sell your personal data to third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Data Retention

  • Account data: Retained while your account is active
  • Prescriptions: Retained for the period required by Indian medical record-keeping laws (minimum 3 years from date of issue)
  • Consultation records: Retained for 3 years from the date of consultation
  • Payment records: Retained for 8 years as required by Indian tax laws
  • Analytics data: Anonymized after 12 months

Your Data Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access — View and download all personal data we hold about you
  • Correct — Update or correct inaccurate personal information
  • Delete — Request deletion of your personal data (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Export — Receive your data in a portable, machine-readable format
  • Restrict processing — Limit how we use your data in certain circumstances

How to Request Data Deletion

  1. In-app: Go to Settings, then Account, then Delete Account. This initiates an immediate deletion request.
  2. Email: Send a deletion request to privacy@rxsecureflow.com from your registered email address.
  3. Google Play: You can also request data deletion through Google Play Store's data deletion options.

What Happens After a Deletion Request

  • Your account is deactivated within 24 hours
  • Personal data is deleted within 30 days
  • Data required for legal compliance (tax records, medical record retention) is retained for the minimum required period, then deleted
  • Anonymized, non-identifiable data may be retained for analytics
  • You will receive a confirmation email once deletion is complete

Children's Data

The Service is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If we discover that a child's data has been collected, we will delete it immediately.

Third-Party Services

The Service uses the following third-party services that may collect data:

  • Razorpay / Cashfree — Payment processing (PCI-DSS compliant)
  • WhatsApp Business API — Prescription delivery (subject to WhatsApp's privacy policy)
  • Convex — Backend data storage and real-time sync
